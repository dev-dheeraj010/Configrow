Everything local business owners usually ask before starting a website. Can't find yours? Call us.

Which plan should I choose?

Standard is our recommended plan: improved UI/UX, extra pages and sections, enhanced SEO, enquiry features and a year of hosting included. Pick Foundation for a clean, essential online presence, or Next-gen for a fully custom 3D website with advanced SEO and integrations.

What is the difference between the three plans?

Foundation (₹10,999 + hosting) covers the essentials: an SEO-friendly structure, responsive layout, essential pages and a contact form. Standard (₹14,549, including free hosting for 1 year) adds improved design, more pages, enhanced SEO and stronger performance. Next-gen (₹19,999, including free hosting for 1 year) is fully custom with advanced SEO, sophisticated features and integrations, and a scalable setup.

Is hosting included?

Standard and Next-gen include free hosting for 1 year. Foundation is priced separately, plus hosting. What happens after the first year: [[add renewal terms]].

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every plan includes a responsive, mobile-friendly layout. Standard is designed mobile-first and Next-gen delivers a high-performance responsive experience.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every plan is built with SEO in mind: a basic SEO-friendly structure in Foundation, an enhanced setup in Standard and advanced integration in Next-gen. Search rankings depend on many things outside anyone's control, so we can't promise a particular position.

What technologies do you use?

HTML, Next.js / Node.js and PHP, chosen to suit your project.
